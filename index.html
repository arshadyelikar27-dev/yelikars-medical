About Yelikar's

Serving the Health of Yenegur with Care & Dedication

Located right opposite Zilla Parishad School, Yenegur, Yelikar's Medical & General Stores has been the neighbourhood's go-to establishment for genuine allopathic and ayurvedic medicines, cosmetics, surgical essentials, and — uniquely — veterinary medicines for your pets and livestock.
